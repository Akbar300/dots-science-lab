# Dot's Science Lab

An animated 3rd grade science practice app with Dot the ladybug. It follows the Virginia 2018 Science SOL and the four FCPS Grade 3 units:

1. Ladybugs (ecosystems, food chains, adaptations, fossils)
2. Water Cycle (reservoirs, the sun's role, states of water, dissolving, conservation)
3. Soils (soil parts, why soil matters, saving soil)
4. Simple Machines (the six machines, forces, friction, gravity)

Features:
- lessons with read-aloud, games, quizzes, word cards and practice tests
- **Dot's Daily 5**: 5-minute spaced review (current unit + older units + missed questions)
- **My Garden**: missed questions grow as sleepy flowers until fixed on 2 different days
- **Kitchen Labs**: 12 hands-on experiments with predict / do / why
- **Test countdown**: daily missions for the days before a test
- **Stump the Grown-up**: write your own questions, which join the Daily 5
- **Grown-ups progress report**: weekly activity, accuracy by topic, class-topic focus, auto read-aloud, printable review sheet

It is one self-contained `index.html` file. Progress is saved in the browser on the device you use (and to your Claude account when opened as a Claude artifact).
