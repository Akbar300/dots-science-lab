# Dot's Science Lab

An animated 3rd grade science practice app with Dot the ladybug. It follows the Virginia 2018 Science SOL and the four FCPS Grade 3 units:

1. Ladybugs (ecosystems, food chains, adaptations, fossils)
2. Water Cycle (reservoirs, the sun's role, states of water, dissolving, conservation)
3. Soils (soil parts, why soil matters, saving soil)
4. Simple Machines (the six machines, forces, friction, gravity)

Features: lessons with read-aloud, games, quizzes, word cards, practice tests, **Dot's Daily 5** spaced review, a **missed-question garden**, and a **grown-ups progress report**.

It is one self-contained `index.html` file. Progress is saved in the browser on the device you use (and to your Claude account when opened as a Claude artifact).
